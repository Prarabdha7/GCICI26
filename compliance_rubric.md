# JA Assure - Global Marketing Compliance Rubric

## 1. Universal Compliance Rules (All Brands)
- No Absolute Guarantees: Assets MUST NOT contain phrases that guarantee claim approval, full payouts, or immediate coverage.
- Accurate Representation of Risk: Assets MUST NOT imply that buying insurance prevents theft, accidents, or lawsuits.
- No Unsubstantiated Competitor Claims: Assets MUST NOT disparage competitors or make unverified superlative claims.

## 2. Brand-Specific Constraints
- DoctorShield: Medical indemnity target. Professional/clinical tone. No promises of immunity from malpractice lawsuits.
- Jade: Jewellers block target. Never detail specific safe grades or alarm types publicly.
- Jaguar Transit: High-value transit target. Never guarantee zero loss or physical invulnerability.

## 3. Mandatory Disclaimers
Every approved asset MUST include at least one disclaimer:
- "Terms, conditions, and exclusions apply."
- "Subject to formal policy wording and underwriting approval."
- "This is general information only and does not constitute financial advice."

## 4. Banned Words
"100% guaranteed", "always approved", "cheap", "loophole", "immune", "foolproof"
