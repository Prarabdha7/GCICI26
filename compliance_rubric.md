# JA Assure - Global Marketing Compliance Rubric

## 1. Universal Compliance Rules (All Brands)
- No Absolute Guarantees: Assets MUST NOT contain phrases that guarantee claim approval, full payouts, or immediate coverage.
- Accurate Representation of Risk: Assets MUST NOT imply that buying insurance prevents theft, accidents, or lawsuits.
- No Unsubstantiated Competitor Claims: Assets MUST NOT disparage competitors or make unverified superlative claims.

## 2. Brand-Specific Constraints
- DoctorShield: Medical indemnity target. Professional/clinical tone. No promises of immunity from malpractice lawsuits.
- Jade: Jewellers block target. Never detail specific safe grades or alarm types publicly.
- Jaguar Transit: High-value transit target. Never guarantee zero loss or physical invulnerability.

## 3. Mandatory Disclaimers
Every approved asset MUST include at least one disclaimer:
- "Terms, conditions, and exclusions apply."
- "Subject to formal policy wording and underwriting approval."
- "This is general information only and does not constitute financial advice."

## 4. Banned Words
"100% guaranteed", "always approved", "cheap", "loophole", "immune", "foolproof", "100% covered", "zero risk", "guaranteed payout", "no questions asked", "unlimited coverage"

## 5. Jurisdictional Schedules (cite as MAS-1, BNM-1, HKIA-1, OIC-1, OJK-1)
- Singapore (MAS Notice 318/321, Insurance Act 1966) [MAS-1]: No guaranteed returns/payouts; must state "This material is for informational purposes and does not constitute insurance or financial advice." Prohibited: "100% covered", "zero risk", "instant guaranteed payout".
- Malaysia (BNM FTFC, Financial Services Act 2013) [BNM-1]: Fair treatment, Bahasa Malaysia clarity where targeted; warning "Maklumat ini adalah untuk tujuan maklumat am sahaja dan tidak membentuk nasihat kewangan rasmi." Prohibited: "dijamin 100%", "tanpa risiko".
- Hong Kong (HKIA Guideline 27, Intermediaries Code) [HKIA-1]: No misleading offer language; must include "本資料僅供參考，並不構成任何要約或保險建議。受條款及細則約束。" Prohibited: "保證賠償", "零風險", "100%賠足".
- Thailand (OIC, Non-Life Insurance Act B.E. 2535) [OIC-1]: Must direct to policy details; warning "เอกสารนี้มีวัตถุประสงค์เพื่อการประชาสัมพันธ์เท่านั้น โปรดศึกษารายละเอียดความคุ้มครอง". Prohibited: "คุ้มครอง 100% ไม่มีเงื่อนไข".
- Indonesia (OJK POJK 22/2023) [OJK-1]: Consumer-protection clarity; warning "Informasi ini hanya untuk edukasi dan bukan merupakan nasihat asuransi formal. Syarat & ketentuan berlaku." Prohibited: "dijamin pasti cair", "tanpa risiko sama sekali".

## 6. Self-Healing Policy (for the gate + UI)
- When a violation is found, propose a minimal redline: replace banned phrase with "subject to underwriting and policy terms" and append the missing disclaimer.
- Violation strings MUST cite the clause (e.g. "Uses 'guaranteed payout' (Rubric 4 / MAS-1)").
- Never silently pass a draft with violations — either FAIL with citations or PASS with disclaimer present.
